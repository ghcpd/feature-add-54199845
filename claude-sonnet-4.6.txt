### 💬 Copilot

**Root cause**: In Python's `re` module, `$` matches at the end of a string **or just before a trailing `\n`**. This caused header values/names ending with `\n` (like `"bar\n"`, `"\n"`, `"foo\n"`) to pass validation even though they contain injection-unsafe newlines.
